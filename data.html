Data Pipeline Layer

AI models are only as trustworthy as the data they're trained on. The data pipeline covers collection, storage, labelling, preprocessing, and ingestion into training. Attacks here corrupt model behaviour invisibly, expose private information through model outputs, or steal valuable proprietary datasets worth years of investment.

Data PoisoningPrivacy LeakageDifferential PrivacyData Lineage
Data Poisoning AttacksCritical

Deliberately injecting malicious or mislabelled training samples to corrupt model behaviour, introduce hidden backdoors, or degrade performance on specific inputs. Can affect any model trained on public, crowd-sourced, or insufficiently controlled data sources.

Technical DetailLabel flipping requires modifying as few as 0.1% of training samples to significantly alter decision boundaries. Gradient-based poisoning computes minimally-perturbed samples that maximally damage model performance. As little as 100 poisoned images in ImageNet-scale datasets can embed reliable backdoors.
Training Data Privacy LeakageHigh

AI models memorise portions of training data and can reproduce personal information, confidential text, or copyrighted material when prompted. GDPR's right to erasure is technically complex when personal data is embedded throughout model weights.

Technical DetailLLMs trained on crawled web data memorise verbatim text including email addresses, phone numbers, and private correspondence. GPT-2 reproduces training data via prefix attack. Diffusion models reproduce near-identical training images. Membership inference attacks determine if specific people's data was in the training set.
Data Supply Chain CompromiseHigh

Attacking upstream data sources that contribute to AI training: web crawlers diverted to malicious pages, third-party data vendors compromised, public datasets (Common Crawl, Wikipedia) poisoned with adversarial content before ingestion.

Technical DetailResearchers found that 6.7% of C4 dataset URLs (used to train GPT-3, T5, LLaMA) were expired and purchasable for ~$10 each. Hosting adversarial content on these domains would retroactively poison all future training runs using Common Crawl — a cheap, scalable attack requiring no access to training pipelines.
Training Data TheftHigh

Proprietary training datasets — medical records, annotated images worth millions, financial transaction data — stolen by insider threats, cloud storage misconfigurations, or external attackers. Stolen datasets directly enable training competing models.

Technical DetailCommon theft vectors: S3/GCS bucket with public ACLs; log files capturing training samples; model APIs that reproduce training data verbatim; annotation vendor with weak security; data science environments with excessive storage permissions.
✅
Data Validation & Integrity
  • 1Implement statistical anomaly detection on incoming training data: distribution shift detection, outlier filtering
  • 2Validate data schema, value ranges, and format for all training datasets before ingestion using Great Expectations
  • 3Compute and verify cryptographic hashes of training dataset files; alert on unexpected changes between training runs
  • 4Use Cleanlab to detect label quality issues and likely mislabels in training sets before training begins

Privacy Protection in Training Data
  • 1Apply Microsoft Presidio or AWS Comprehend PII to scrub personal information before training data ingestion
  • 2Use Differential Privacy during training: TensorFlow Privacy or PyTorch Opacus with DP-SGD (target ε < 10)
  • 3Implement data minimisation: only collect and retain training data necessary for the AI task
  • 4Conduct regular membership inference audits to measure actual privacy leakage from deployed models

2016BreachCritical
Microsoft Tay Chatbot Data Poisoning

Within 24 hours of launch, coordinated Twitter users deliberately taught Microsoft's Tay chatbot to produce harmful content through sustained adversarial interaction. Tay's real-time learning mechanism treated adversarial inputs as legitimate training signal. Microsoft took Tay offline within 16 hours.

ImpactSignificant reputational damage to Microsoft. Forced complete rethink of online learning systems and adversarial user interaction in AI products worldwide.
Key LessonReal-time learning systems require poisoning detection, rate limiting on input influence, and human review of learning updates. Online learning is extremely high-risk for consumer-facing AI.
2023BreachHigh
Samsung Employee Data Leaks via ChatGPT

Multiple Samsung engineers pasted proprietary chip fabrication code, internal meeting notes, and production source code into ChatGPT for debugging help. Samsung discovered the leaks and banned ChatGPT companywide. The data may have become part of OpenAI's training corpus.

ImpactProprietary semiconductor IP potentially ingested by a competitor-accessible AI model. Samsung issued emergency AI usage policy. Three incidents occurred within a single month.
Key LessonCorporate AI acceptable use policies are essential before deployment. DLP tools must monitor text submitted to external AI APIs.
2020ResearchHigh
GPT-2 Training Data Verbatim Extraction

Google researchers demonstrated extracting verbatim memorised training data from GPT-2 using specific prefix prompts. Recovered content included full names, email addresses, phone numbers, and private text passages — proving that LLMs are leaky archives of their training data.

ImpactForced model providers to implement differential privacy and training data deduplication in subsequent models. Established that model outputs can violate user privacy at training time.
Key LessonDifferential privacy during training and aggressive deduplication are essential for models trained on personal data.
TensorFlow Privacy
Implements DP-SGD for differentially private training in TensorFlow; includes RDP accountant for privacy budget tracking.
Opacus (PyTorch)
PyTorch library for training with differential privacy; supports per-sample gradients and DP-SGD.
Microsoft Presidio
PII detection and anonymisation for text training data; supports 50+ entity types across multiple languages.
Great Expectations
Data validation framework; define and enforce expectations for training data quality and statistical properties.
DVC
Data Version Control: version training datasets alongside model code; track data lineage and provenance.
Cleanlab
Automated label error detection using confident learning; finds likely mislabels in AI training datasets.
