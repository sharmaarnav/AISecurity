Identity & Access Layer

AI is transforming identity attacks. Generative AI enables high-fidelity voice and video deepfakes that bypass biometric authentication. AI-powered password spraying and credential stuffing operate at machine speed. Synthetic identities created from AI-generated data defeat KYC systems. At the same time, AI offers powerful new defences in behavioural biometrics and anomaly detection.

Deepfake AuthCredential AttacksAI ImpersonationSynthetic Identity
Deepfake-Based Authentication BypassCritical

AI-generated face and voice deepfakes defeat facial recognition and voice-based authentication systems. Real-time face-swap technology streams convincing video during live verification calls. As biometric authentication proliferates, deepfake attacks on authentication become the highest-impact identity threat vector.

Technical DetailDiffusion model-based face generation and GAN-based face swapping produce images that pass liveness detection checks designed for injection attack prevention. Voice cloning from as little as 3 seconds of audio (ElevenLabs, XTTS) replicates voice patterns with high fidelity. Video call deepfakes (DeepFaceLive, similar tools) operate at 30fps in real-time. Injection attacks pipe synthetic frames directly into the camera feed, bypassing passive liveness checks entirely.
AI-Powered Credential AttacksHigh

Machine learning models trained on breached credential datasets generate statistically likely password candidates with far higher hit rates than rule-based wordlists. AI dramatically increases the efficiency of password spraying, credential stuffing, and targeted account takeover attacks.

Technical DetailPassGAN uses GANs to generate password candidates matching real-world password distributions. Hashcat rule-based attacks are being replaced with ML-generated candidate lists that achieve 51–73% crack rates on leaked datasets in benchmarks. LLM-powered phishing generates personalised, grammatically perfect lures from scraped social media — dramatically increasing click rates on credential-harvesting pages compared to generic phishing templates.
AI Impersonation at ScaleHigh

AI enables impersonation campaigns at a scale and quality previously impossible: automated spear-phishing emails personalised per target, AI voice calls mimicking executives for BEC fraud, and AI-generated social media personas that build trust over months before executing social engineering attacks.

Technical DetailBusiness Email Compromise (BEC) enhanced with voice deepfakes: attackers clone the CEO's voice from LinkedIn videos and call the CFO requesting emergency wire transfers. LLM-generated spear-phishing emails reference specific projects, colleagues, and recent meetings scraped from LinkedIn and company blogs — a task that previously required manual research per target but now runs at pipeline scale targeting thousands per day.
Synthetic Identity FraudHigh

Attackers create fictional identities from AI-generated photos, synthesised documents, and fabricated credit histories that pass automated KYC (Know Your Customer) checks. Synthetic identity fraud is the fastest-growing financial crime, estimated to cost US financial institutions over $6 billion annually.

Technical DetailAI-generated face images (ThisPersonDoesNotExist.com technology) are used as profile photos for synthetic identities that pass basic document verification. Generative AI creates convincing counterfeit utility bills and bank statements. Synthetic Social Security Numbers are combined with real personal details to build "Frankenstein" identities that survive credit checks. Automated account creation bots use these identities to open bank accounts, obtain credit, and commit fraud before detection.

Deepfake-Resistant Authentication
  • 1Deploy active liveness detection with unpredictable challenge-response (random head movements, blinking sequences) resistant to injection attacks
  • 2Implement hardware-bound authentication (passkeys, FIDO2) that is immune to deepfake attacks — biometrics unlock the device, not the service
  • 3Use AI deepfake detection models (Intel FakeCatcher, Microsoft Video Authenticator) as an additional layer on video verification flows
  • 4For high-value transactions, require in-person verification or hardware token — never rely solely on voice or video biometrics

Voice & BEC Fraud Prevention
  • 1Establish out-of-band verbal confirmation protocols for wire transfers above threshold amounts; never approve via email alone
  • 2Train finance staff to recognise AI voice cloning tells: slight latency, unnatural prosody, inability to engage in spontaneous conversation
  • 3Deploy AI-powered email security tools that score for BEC indicators: new sender, urgency, financial request, spoofed domain
  • 4Use shared secret code words for phone-based financial authorisation requests — a pre-agreed phrase that deepfakes cannot know

2024BreachCritical
$25M Deepfake Video Call Finance Fraud — Arup

A finance employee at engineering firm Arup was tricked into transferring HK$200 million (~$25 million USD) after attending a video conference call where every other participant — including the "CFO" — was a deepfake. The employee had initial doubts but was convinced by the realistic video and audio of colleagues. Arrests were made in Hong Kong.

Impact$25 million USD lost. Largest publicly disclosed deepfake fraud incident to date. Demonstrated that multi-person deepfake video calls are operationally feasible for sophisticated fraud groups. Prompted global financial institutions to revise wire transfer authorisation procedures.
Key LessonNo financial transaction above a defined threshold should be authorised solely based on video call participation. Out-of-band verification via a known trusted phone number is mandatory. Deepfake-resistant confirmation codes must be established in advance.
2023BreachHigh
AI Voice Clone Used in Bank Account Takeover

Fraudsters cloned the voice of a bank customer from social media video recordings and called the bank's automated voice authentication system. The voice clone successfully passed voice biometric verification, enabling the attacker to access account details and initiate transfers. The bank's voice authentication vendor confirmed the system was defeated by the synthetic voice.

ImpactAccount takeover via voice biometric bypass. Multiple financial institutions using the same voice authentication platform were potentially vulnerable. Led to emergency reviews of voice biometric security across the banking sector.
Key LessonVoice biometrics alone are insufficient for high-value transaction authentication. Voice authentication must be combined with additional factors (OTP, passkey) and active liveness challenges that require real-time spontaneous response.
2022ResearchHigh
PassGAN: AI Password Cracking Exceeds Rule-Based Tools

Home Security Heroes demonstrated that PassGAN, a GAN-based password generation tool trained on the RockYou2021 leak, could crack 51% of common passwords in under a minute and 71% within a day — significantly outperforming traditional rule-based wordlist attacks for complex password patterns. The research highlighted the urgent need to deprecate short passwords entirely.

ImpactDemonstrated AI makes any password under 8 characters with predictable patterns crackable in minutes. Changed industry minimum password length recommendations. Accelerated adoption of passkeys and passwordless authentication as the only robust defence.
Key LessonPassword-based authentication is approaching end-of-life against AI-powered attacks. Transition to passkeys (FIDO2) or long randomly generated passwords (20+ chars) stored in a password manager. MFA remains essential as a compensating control.
FIDO2 / Passkeys
Hardware-bound, phishing-resistant authentication standard that is immune to deepfake attacks — the credential never leaves the device.
BioCatch
Behavioural biometrics platform providing continuous identity verification via typing, mouse, and touchscreen patterns — detects bot and account takeover in real-time.
Intel FakeCatcher
Real-time deepfake video detection using blood flow analysis (photoplethysmography) — detects synthetic faces that lack real physiological signals.
Socure
AI-powered identity verification platform using document analysis, biometrics, and network graph signals to detect synthetic identities in KYC flows.
Microsoft Entra ID Protection
ML-based risk detection for Azure AD: impossible travel, leaked credentials, anonymous IP use, atypical travel patterns, and password spray detection.
Sardine
Fraud and compliance platform using device intelligence and behaviour-based signals to catch synthetic identity fraud in financial onboarding flows.
